\subsubsection{Stranded Mass}
By default the mobile domain scales both the dissolved and the sorbed solute mass by the saturation of the cell, so when a cell drains the solute that the cell held is returned to the water that remains and the simulated concentration rises.  Solute held by water that does not drain, and solute held by the solid phase, does not move with the water table, and the STRANDED\_MASS option holds that mass out of the mobile domain until the cell rewets.

The mass held out of the mobile domain when a cell drains is the solute in the residual water, which is the volume of water given by RESIDUAL\_WATER\_CONTENT, plus all of the sorbed mass, because the solid phase does not drain.  With RESIDUAL\_WATER\_CONTENT set to zero, which is the default, only sorbed mass is held out of the mobile domain; this requires no additional input and is the recommended starting point.  Stranded mass is not transported and takes no part in dispersion.

When a cell rewets, the stranded mass is assumed to be distributed uniformly through the unsaturated part of the cell, and the share of it held by the newly wetted volume is returned to the mobile domain.  The mass that is returned is the mass that was held out of the mobile domain by the same cell, so no mass is created or destroyed and no neighboring cell is used.  A cell that drains completely and then rewets completely recovers all of the mass it gave up; a cell that rewets part of the way recovers only the corresponding share, because the mass in the part that stays dry stays there.

Stranded mass decays at the rates given by DECAY and DECAY\_SORBED, so that solute in each phase decays at the rate that belongs to that phase.  Decay of stranded mass never passes through the concentration equation, so it is reported in a separate budget for the package rather than in the model budget; only the transfer between the mobile domain and the stranded mass, which is reported as STORAGE-STRANDED, appears in the model budget.  Stranded mass does not decay when zero-order decay is active, because zero-order decay would drive an empty reservoir negative.

The STRANDED\_MASS option cannot be used together with the Immobile Storage and Transfer (IST) Package.  An immobile domain also scales its mass by the saturation of the cell, so it would return its solute to the mobile domain on drainage while the mobile domain held its own mass out, and the two domains would be inconsistent with one another.
